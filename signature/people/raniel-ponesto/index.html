Image signature: looks identical in every email app, light or dark mode, on phone and desktop.

  1. Click Copy signature.
  2. Outlook: Settings → Accounts → Signatures (Classic Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures).
  3. Delete the old signature completely, paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V), and save.
  4. Send a test email to yourself and check it on your phone.

Every part of the card is an image, so email apps can't change its colours, font or layout. Each part stays clickable: logo and website, address (map), WhatsApp, and the four social icons.
