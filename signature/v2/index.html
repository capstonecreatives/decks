Add it to Outlook

  1. Click Download signature (GIF).
  2. Outlook → Settings → Signatures → select your signature and delete everything in it.
  3. Drag the downloaded GIF into the signature box (or Picture icon → Picture from File).
  4. Click the image, press Cmd+K, paste https://capstoneproperty.com, and save.
